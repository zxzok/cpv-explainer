Taken together, the pooled Sleep and AF analyses provide clearer evidence for
differences between pre-specified dispersed and contiguous layouts than for
differences among learned supports, although the Sleep
fixed-template effects remain heterogeneous across source studies. The
learned supports vary across targets and resamples, and their paired held-out
$R^2$ differences span zero. The subsampling scheme and additional weighting
and regularisation diagnostics are given in
