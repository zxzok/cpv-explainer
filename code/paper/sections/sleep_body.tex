For REM, \cref{fig:real}(a) shows that the centred-contiguous template has
cross-fitted $R^2$ values \numCfContigFour, \numCfContigSixteen{} and
\numCfContigSixtyFour{} at $N=4,16,64$, compared with
\numCfUniformFour, \numCfUniformSixteen{} and \numCfUniformSixtyFour{} for
uniform dispersion. Across the 15 target--budget cells, the conditional
dispersed-minus-contiguous range has a positive lower endpoint in
\numSleepAdjustedPositiveRanges{} cells.

The pooled analysis therefore favours dispersion at several budgets, but the
source-specific estimates are heterogeneous. When SC and ST are analysed by
separate subject-grouped pipelines, \numSleepCohortPositiveRanges{} of
\numSleepCohortCells{} source--target--budget ranges lie above zero,
\numSleepCohortNegativeRanges{} lie below zero and
\numSleepCohortUnresolvedRanges{} include zero. These source-specific and
fixed-template sensitivity procedures are detailed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-real-sensitivity}.
