\subsection{Retrospective Protocol Comparison}
\label{sec:real}

Using complete expert annotations, we reconstruct the measurements that each
partial protocol would have produced and evaluate fitted predictors on
held-out units. Sleep protocols sample binary stage indicators from scored
hypnograms, whereas AF protocols average binary rhythm annotations within
selected time bins. Each reconstructed protocol is therefore defined by the
subset or aggregation of annotation times made available to the predictor.

Sleep contains \numSleepRecordings{} recordings from \numSleepSubjects{}
subjects and uses $p=\numSleepGrid$ relative-time anchors. Its targets are the
exact full-interval REM, N3 and Wake proportions, and its budgets
$N\in\{4,8,16,32,64\}$ count distinct scored 30-second epochs. Long-Term AF
contains \numLtafRecords{} records, uses $p=\numAfGrid$ equal relative-time
bins and targets the exact fraction of analysable annotated time spent in AF.
Its budgets $N\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$ observe the fraction $N/p$ of each record.
Both analyses use five outer folds and report pooled held-out cross-fitted
$R^2$. Sleep folds are subject-disjoint and stratified by source study; AF
folds are split by record because repeated-subject identifiers are unavailable.

Two fixed templates are compared at every budget: a centred-contiguous block
and anchors dispersed as uniformly as possible over relative time. Sleep also
learns target-aware and kernel-quadrature supports entirely within each outer
training fold; AF uses only the pre-specified templates. The learned Sleep
procedure uses a regularised best-linear score for foldwise protocol selection
and is evaluated by held-out cross-fitted $R^2$; \cref{sec:estimation}
analyses the corresponding fixed-dimensional Gaussian plug-in setting.
Residual covariance, target cross-covariance and target variance are estimated
foldwise from the annotations, with an eigenvalue floor adapted to the
high-dimensional grid.
The common-grid mapping, baselines, weighting, selection score, nested ridge
fitting and resampling procedures are specified in
Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-real}.

Sleep-EDF Expanded \citep{kemp2000analysis,mourtazaev1995age,
goldberger2000physiobank,pollard2026physionet} combines Sleep Cassette (SC) and
Sleep Telemetry (ST) 30-second hypnograms. SC contributes
\numSleepScRecords{} recordings from \numSleepScSubjects{} participants and ST
contributes \numSleepStRecords{} recordings from \numSleepStSubjects{}
participants. Stages 3 and 4 are combined as N3
\citep{rechtschaffen1968manual,iber2007aasm}. The retained records contain
\numSleepHours{} valid annotated hours.
